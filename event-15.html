Entrepreneurship • Category 15

Techeneurship

AI, FinTech & Frontier Technology Ventures

Commercialize deep-tech, artificial intelligence, blockchain, or SaaS innovations. Focus on technical feasibility, IP defensibility, and scalable cloud architecture.

Competition Rounds & Structure

Round 1: Deep Tech Architecture Blueprint

Present software/hardware architecture diagrams, IP strategy, and target tech market.

Round 2: Live Prototype / API Demo

Demonstrate live working code, API integration, or algorithmic model.

Round 3: Enterprise Pitch & Growth Model

Pitch enterprise sales pipeline, ARR growth, and cloud scalability.

Essential Frameworks & Concepts

SaaS Magic Number

(Quarter Net New ARR * 4) / Sales & Marketing Expenditure. Target > 0.75.

Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

(Starting ARR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Starting ARR.

Cloud Microservices Architecture

Decoupled API-first infrastructure design.

Official Scoring Rubric

Evaluation Criteria Weightage
Technical Feasibility & Innovation 35%
SaaS Metrics & Commercial Model 35%
Live Demo & Pitch Defense 30%

Master Strategy Pro Tip

Translate complex technical algorithms into clear business ROI. Investors buy business outcomes.

Recommended Preparation Challenge

Architect an AI-powered financial fraud detection platform pitch including cloud architecture diagrams and API monetization.
